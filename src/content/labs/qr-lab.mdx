Build a code, style it, and export it in your browser.

There is nothing to install and nothing to sign up for. Choose **Use here** to try it without leaving this
page, or choose **Open separately** for the full-window app.

The QR generator covers URLs, plain text, WiFi joining details, contact cards and calendar events. You can
set the error-correction level for print, drop in a logo or watermark, and export as PNG or SVG.
