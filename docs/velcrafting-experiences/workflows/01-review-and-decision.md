## Discovery questions

- What decision is currently blocked, and what would make it easier to decide?
- Which outcomes matter most? Which constraints are fixed, negotiable or still unknown?
- What has already been tried, accepted or rejected? Where are those decisions recorded?
- Which parts of the system can be inspected? What remains unavailable?
- Who can clarify facts, approve recommendations and authorize subsequent work?
- What level of evidence is proportionate to this decision?

## Risks and change triggers

Revisit scope when the reviewed system changes materially, important evidence becomes available, access is withdrawn, or the requested decision expands. Record the proposed change, its effect on the assessment and the person authorized to decide. Preserve the earlier scope and report versions. Further investigation starts only within the applicable agreed authority.
